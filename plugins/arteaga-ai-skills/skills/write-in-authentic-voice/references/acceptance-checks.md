# Acceptance checks

Apply the relevant checks to the actual output. Revise failures before delivering; do not print this checklist by default.

## All modes

- The result answers the request for the intended author, audience, and channel.
- Every material fact, citation, exact term, uncertainty, and qualification is preserved. No unsupported personal detail, number, source, opinion, or commitment is added.
- Voice evidence is appropriate to this author and context; a private personal profile has not leaked into organizational writing.
- Pattern guidance improves the writing without banning accurate words, erasing character, or forcing uniform rhythm.
- No private history was accessed or external communication sent merely because writing was requested.

## Draft

- With examples, repeated voice signals guide decisions without copying distinctive sentences or exaggerating quirks.
- Without examples, the draft uses supplied context conservatively and does not block on a sample unless it is essential.
- The user receives usable prose rather than an unrequested outline or style explanation.

## Edit

- Strong sentences and useful structure remain intact; changes are proportional to actual problems.
- Intentional fragments, technical terminology, humor, useful repetition, and honest uncertainty survive.
- Change notes are optional. Copy-only output contains only the finished copy.

## Audit

- Findings identify specific patterns, minimal quotations, and focused fixes.
- The response does not rewrite, invent problems to fill a list, score AI likelihood, or infer authorship.
