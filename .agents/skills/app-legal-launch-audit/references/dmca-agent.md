# DMCA agent

Use this check only if the app stores user-posted content and the operator wants the 17 U.S.C. 512(c) safe harbor. Skip it, and mark it not applicable, for an app with no user content.

Registration is an external Copyright Office step. Walk the user through it. Do not submit the designation unless the user explicitly asks and supplies the agent details.

## What to verify

- The service has a designated agent in the Copyright Office directory: https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/
- The same agent contact is published on the site, usually in the footer or a copyright policy.
- The designation has not expired.

## Walkthrough

1. Open https://dmca.copyright.gov/ and sign in or create a Copyright Office account.
2. Register a service provider designation. Paper designations are not accepted.
3. Enter the service provider name, the agent name, a physical address, phone, and email. The directory listing is public.
4. Pay the current fee. The Copyright Office lists $6 per designation; confirm the amount on the registration page before paying.
5. Submit and save the receipt. A designation expires in 3 years unless it is amended or resubmitted.
6. Publish the agent name, address, phone, and email on the site. Directory registration alone does not finish the step.

A registered agent is not a repeat-infringer policy and not a takedown workflow. If those are missing, mention them as out of scope rather than building them here.
