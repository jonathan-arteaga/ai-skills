# Renewal terms

Fail if a paid plan renews automatically and the renewal terms are not in visual proximity to the subscribe or consent control, before the charge.

California Business and Professions Code section 17602 requires clear and conspicuous renewal terms in visual proximity to the request for consent. Other states and the Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act also expect clear terms and affirmative consent. Do not treat a terms link alone as the disclosure.

## Pass

Immediately next to the subscribe button, before charge, the customer can see:

- the price charged now
- the billing interval
- that the plan renews until canceled
- the post-trial price and when the first renewal charge happens, if there is a trial
- how to cancel

Consent is an affirmative action. A prechecked box fails. Keep the consent evidence the billing flow already stores; do not invent a new legal archive.

## Fix

Put the terms in the same view as the button, not only in a modal the customer can skip or a linked policy. Match the processor amount and interval to the copy. If the provider checkout hides the app's copy, report that as still failing.
