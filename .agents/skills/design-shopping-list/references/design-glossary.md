## Words for the feel

| You might say | Designer term | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frosted, see-through panels | Glassmorphism | Translucent surface with blur behind it. |
| Grainy, film-like texture | Grain, noise | Fine speckle over flat color to add depth. |
| Soft blended colors | Gradient, mesh gradient | Colors fading into each other; mesh uses several points. |
| Glow behind something | Radial glow, backdrop gradient | Light spot behind an element to draw the eye. |
| Rounded or sharp corners | Corner radius | How round the corners of boxes and buttons are. |
| Things that look lifted | Elevation, shadow | Shadow depth that makes a surface feel raised. |
| Lots of space vs. packed | Density, whitespace | How much room surrounds content. |
| Bold, strong difference | Contrast | Difference between text and background, or between sizes. |
| Headline much bigger than body | Type scale | The set of text sizes and how far apart they are. |
| Fancy vs. clean letters | Serif vs. sans-serif | Serifs have small strokes at letter ends; sans do not. |
| Code-looking text accents | Monospace | Every letter the same width. |
| Small reactions when you click or hover | Micro-interaction, hover state | Feedback animation on a single control. |
| Bouncy movement | Spring motion | Motion that overshoots slightly and settles. |
| Background moves slower than the page | Parallax | Layers scrolling at different speeds. |
| Things appear as you scroll | Scroll reveal | Elements fade or slide in on entering the screen. |
| Dark background, light text | Dark mode | Dark color scheme; needs its own contrast checks. |
| Calm and simple | Minimal | Few colors, generous space, restrained motion. |
| Busy and loud | Maximal, expressive | Many colors, textures, and motion at once. |
